We are grateful God is at work! Here's a look at what's coming up at First Baptist Church Muncie from now through Easter Sunday, April 5, 2026. Mark your calendars and invite a friend!

Trial & Testimony – Lenten Series 2026

This Lenten season, we're walking through a powerful series called Trial & Testimony about how God is at work in the trials and difficulties in our lives, not just when everything seems bright and beautiful.

Events run through Holy Week — see below for specific dates and details.

Men's Lenten Breakfast

Saturday, March 7 · 9:00 AM · Hosted at FBC Muncie this week (other Saturdays rotate to ABC sister churches). A great morning of food, fellowship, and faith. All men are welcome! Register here.

Serve Your City Day

Saturday, March 14 · 8:30 AM Breakfast & Prayer at Church · 9:00 AM – Noon Community Service. Come ready to roll up your sleeves and love our city! Register here.

Easter Eggventure – Calling All Eggsplorers! 🥚

Saturday, March 28 · 11:30 AM Lunch · Noon Indoor Egg Hunt · 12:30 PM Craft. A fun family event for kids — bring the little ones and get egg-cited!

Invite a Friend Sunday / Palm Sunday

Sunday, March 29 · 10:45 AM. Who in your life needs to hear the Good News? Bring them with you as we kick off Holy Week and celebrate Jesus' triumphal entry into Jerusalem!

Good Friday Worship @ The Garden

Friday, April 3 · 7:00 PM · Jesus' Seven Last Words. Join us for a reverent and moving Good Friday service at The Garden of Gethsemane. A night to pause, reflect, and draw near to Christ.

Easter Worship 🌸

Sunday, April 5 · 10:45 AM. He is risen! Come celebrate Easter with us. Bring flowers to help decorate the cross as we worship the risen Christ together!

Bring flowers to decorate the cross!

For more information on any of these events, visit FBCmuncie.org or check the Church Center App.
