A wedding at First Baptist

First Baptist Church is a fitting wedding location and the facilities can be reserved for Christian weddings.

Building rentals for this purpose is something we do to share our building with sisters and brothers in Christ in the area so they might celebrate at a reasonable cost. Any associated fees are used exclusively for the cost of utilities, maintaining our building, and accounting for the time of our wedding coordinator, and others, who help make wedding ceremonies possible.

If you are interested, please read the following information and requirements for using our building and decide if our church may be right for your wedding ceremony.

Wedding Coordinator

Ella Mae Lemen

Ella Mae is a long-time member of First Baptist Church who also serves as our Wedding Coordinator. She is the appropriate person to contact if you are interested in celebrating a Christian wedding ceremony at First Baptist.

Using the building for other events

Throughout our history and into the present, First Baptist has opened up its facilities for use by friends and community organizations.

If you would like to use our building on a short term basis in some other way, you will need to complete a building usage form. Please fill out an online Building Use Request Form. If the online building request form is filled out, only the signed Property Use Agreement (Release and Indemnity Agreement / Acceptance of Responsibility) must be signed and turned into the office, and you will not be required to fill out all the information about your event by hand. Those who do not fill out the online form will need to fill out the entire building use form by hand and turn it into the Church Office, before the building use request can be considered.
