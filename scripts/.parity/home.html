1859&1929

Our Building

First Baptist’s third, and most recent, building was completed in 1929. It has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1988.

  1. 1859

    Twelve Indiana residents, meeting at the county courthouse, founded the first Baptist Church in Muncie.

  2. 1862

    The tiny congregation moved into their new church building.

  3. 1890

    The congregation eventually moved into a new building on July 20, 1890.

  4. 1929

    On May 12, 1929, 1200 people marched from the old church to the new one on the corner of Jefferson and Adams streets.

  5. 2026

    First Baptist Muncie continues to discern God’s will to serve in a new era in the life of Muncie.
