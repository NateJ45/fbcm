# Job Listing: Pianist / Organist (Part-Time)

![cover](../images/81f7ac_dd1a6e26262c403daf710e8047e1a496~mv2.png)

**First Baptist Church, Muncie**

Downloadable and Printable Pianist / Organist Job Listing At File Above.

**Applications will be accepted until October 14th, 2025.**

#### Overview

First Baptist Church Muncie is seeking a part-time **Pianist/ Organist\***to accompany our Worship Arts Pastor in Christ-centered, intergenerational worship. We’re looking for someone with a servant’s heart, musical ability, and a desire to guide the church in meaningful and Spirit-led worship that honors both tradition and innovation.

We currently enjoy a blended style of worship, drawing from both contemporary Christian music and traditional hymnody. Our ideal candidate values collaboration and is passionate about creating a worship atmosphere that draws people into God’s presence across all generations.

\* *Piano skills required, both instruments preferred, but not required, for application.*

##### Reports To

- Worship Arts Pastor

##### Hours & Compensation

- **Hours:** 2–4 hours per week
- **Compensation:** $150 per week
- **Vacation:** 4 weeks

#### Key Responsibilities

###### Pianist / Organist

- Provide piano and/or organ music for weekly worship services (including prelude, hymns, offertory, communion, postlude, and special music as needed).
- Church has a 22-Rank Rodgers Pipe Organ
- Accompany the congregation, soloists, and/or choir during worship.
- Attend and provide music for rehearsals or special services (funerals, weddings, holiday services) as agreed upon and compensated separately if outside normal hours.
- Coordinate with the Co-Pastors and Worship Arts Pastor to select appropriate music.
- Maintain the church’s piano and organ areas in an orderly manner and report any maintenance needs.

###### Qualifications & Expectations

- Proficiency in piano and/or organ, with ability to play hymns and worship music in a church setting.
- Familiarity with American Baptist worship traditions and hymnody (preferred).
- Ability to collaborate effectively with the Pastor, Worship Team, and other musicians.
- Reliability and punctuality for scheduled services and rehearsals.
