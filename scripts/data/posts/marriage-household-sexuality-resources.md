# Marriage, Household & Sexuality Resources

![cover](../images/81f7ac_6c02a947d59b4d33ae778bc935c0b6d7~mv2.png)

*Resources this week focus on marriage, “headship,” mutually serving one another in marriage and how marriage involves an exclusive commitment of husband and wife, and also opens up hospitality to, and is supported by, the whole body of Christ.*

Resources for week 3 of the "Kingdom Family Vacation" series entitled "Turning Headship On Its Head'"
