# The Missing Piece

![cover](../images/08181c_1c05a295546247c2acfe971dd8b9bc4d~mv2.jpg)

This week we have a guest preacher. Our region minister, Rev. Rusty Strickler. [As always, our livestreams can be found online here](https://fbcmuncie.churchcenter.com/channels/4999).

**"So Peter was kept in prison, but the church was earnestly praying to God for him."**

**-excerpt from Acts 12:1-19**

Ever tried to assemble a puzzle and then realize a piece is missing?  Frustration! There's a missing piece in our American churches today.  All the other pieces are present: uplifting worship services and music, preaching rich in depth and knowledge, programs and ministries.

Yet, there is a missing piece when put into place will create a habitation for Holy Spirit to awaken the church and bring revival.  Come Sunday and learn how you may help insert this missing piece back into FBCM!
