# Job Listing: Worship Arts Pastor (Part-time)

![cover](../images/81f7ac_612ebbc86d2a4d208e7965dbda2d986a~mv2.png)

**First Baptist Church, Muncie**

Downloadable and Printable Worship Arts Pastor Job Listing At File Above.

**Applications will be accepted until October 14th, 2025.**

#### **Overview**

First Baptist Church Muncie is seeking a part-time **Worship Arts Pastor** to lead our congregation in Christ-centered, intergenerational worship. We’re looking for someone with a servant’s heart, musical ability, and a desire to guide the church in meaningful and Spirit-led worship that honors both tradition and innovation.

We currently enjoy a blended style of worship, drawing from both contemporary Christian music and traditional hymnody. Our ideal candidate values collaboration and is passionate about creating a worship atmosphere that draws people into God’s presence across all generations.

##### Reports To

Co-Pastors

##### Hours & Compensation

- **Hours:** 10-13 hours per week
- **Compensation:**Up to $20,000 annually, with flexibility to divide between salary, housing allowance, and insurance benefits based on the candidate’s needs.
- **Vacation:** 4 weeks

#### **Key Responsibilities**

##### Worship Leadership

- Lead & model worship to our congregation during Sunday morning service
- Communicate and rehearse with worship volunteers (musicians, vocalists, and tech team)
- Collaborate with those managing sound, slides, and livestream
- Support special services and church events as needed
- Maintain a flexible schedule for rehearsals, services, and seasonal events
- Assist with accurate and timely CCLI reporting

##### Worship Planning

- Stay informed on biblically based practices in both contemporary and traditional worship music
- Recruit, schedule, and support volunteers for music and tech ministries.
- Prepare rehearsal materials and introduce new songs to the congregation
- Assist in finalizing music information for ProPresenter slides
- Collaborate with other ministry departments to ensure cohesive programming

##### Team Development

- Cultivate a healthy, encouraging environment for music and tech volunteers

##### Team Development (continued)

- Maintain open communication with church leadership, staff, and the congregation about worship ministry initiatives
- Attend weekly staff meetings with the pastors and annual CCT planning retreats

##### Qualifications & Expectations

- A growing, active relationship with Jesus Christ
- Comfortable working within the theological framework of the American Baptist Churches USA
- Demonstrated musical proficiency (either vocal or instrumental required; both are ideal)
- Familiarity with both traditional hymns and contemporary worship music
- Experience working with or leading volunteer teams
- Strong communication and organizational skills
- Dependable, flexible, and collaborative in nature
- Familiarity with worship technology (e.g., ProPresenter, Planning Center, livestreaming, sound systems) is a plus
- Bachelor degree preferred, or a minimum of 5 years relevant experience

#### About First Baptist Church Muncie

First Baptist Church Muncie is a Spirit-led people gathered to join Christ’s presence in our community. Since 1859, we’ve been making disciples and serving our neighbors in downtown Muncie. We are affiliated with the **American Baptist Churches USA** and the **American Baptist Churches of Indiana & Kentucky**.
