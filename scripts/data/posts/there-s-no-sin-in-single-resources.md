# There's No 'Sin' in 'Single' Resources

![cover](../images/81f7ac_fae9ef9da3944b5f9abb7e8d3cf8a60d~mv2.png)

*Resources this week focus on singleness, divorce, being a widow / widower, and how single and married, childless and parents, are alike whole people participating in God’s Kingdom.*

Resources for week 2 of the "Kingdom Family Vacation" series entitled "There's No 'Sin' In 'Single'"

- [Sermon Preview blog](https://www.fbcmuncie.org/post/there-s-no-sin-in-single).
