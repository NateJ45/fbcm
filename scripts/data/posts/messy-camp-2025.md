# Messy Camp 2025

![cover](../images/81f7ac_ea52ef4eb7ea4e289efde3bb0aa5adaa~mv2.png)

![ Last Year's Outdoor Carnival. Snow cones, balloon animals, crafts, games and more!](../images/81f7ac_b0ff139891fe4826ac092aa90afcb0d4~mv2.png)

Last Year's Outdoor Carnival. Snow cones, balloon animals, crafts, games and more!

Join us for a week of ALL the MESSY foods, games, stories, and crafts that your kids love! Messy Camp is designed to give kids a week of safe sensory play that the parents don't have to clean up. It's a win-win for kids and their families!

**Messy Camp is Sunday, July 13 through Wednesday, July 16, from 6-8PM.**

Activities are available to children 4 years old through just finished 5th grade.  Come through the Jefferson street side or circular drive entrances to find the registration table near our Welcome Center.

**Thursday night, July 17, at 6:00PM** will be a parking lot party for the WHOLE family to enjoy together. There will be free hotdogs and family activity stations.

You can register [at this link](https://fbcmuncie.churchcenter.com/registrations/events/3023179).

Register By Pressing / Clicking Here

For questions, please contact [children@fbcmuncie.org](mailto:children@fbcmuncie.org).

Invite a friend! We can't wait to see you there.
