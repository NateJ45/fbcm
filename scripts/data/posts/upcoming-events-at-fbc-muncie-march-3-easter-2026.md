We are grateful God is at work! Here's a look at what's coming up at First Baptist Church Muncie from now through Easter Sunday, April 5, 2026. Mark your calendars and invite a friend!

### Trial & Testimony – Lenten Series 2026

This Lenten season, we're walking through a powerful series called Trial & Testimony about how God is at work in the trials and difficulties in our lives, not just when everything seems bright and beautiful.[http://beautiful.Events](http://beautiful.Events)

[Events](http://beautiful.Events) run through Holy Week — see below for specific dates and details.

#### Men's Lenten Breakfast

Saturday, March 7 · 9:00 AM · Hosted at FBC Muncie this week (other Saturdays rotate to ABC sister churches). A great morning of food, fellowship, and faith. All men are welcome! [Register here.](https://fbcmuncie.churchcenter.com/registrations/events/3488185)

![](../images/81f7ac_5d54845d75eb455cac79bd22fde1aee2~mv2.png)

#### Serve Your City Day

Saturday, March 14 · 8:30 AM Breakfast & Prayer at Church · 9:00 AM – Noon Community Service. Come ready to roll up your sleeves and love our city! [Register here.](https://fbcmuncie.churchcenter.com/registrations/events/3488178)

![](../images/81f7ac_5dfccefa00574f48a79889cfdda78a5d~mv2.png)

#### Easter Eggventure – Calling All Eggsplorers! 🥚

Saturday, March 28 · 11:30 AM Lunch · Noon Indoor Egg Hunt · 12:30 PM Craft. A fun family event for kids — bring the little ones and get egg-cited!

![](../images/81f7ac_c5178eec3a8b49c0b8d960188dada663~mv2.png)

#### Invite a Friend Sunday / Palm Sunday

Sunday, March 29 · 10:45 AM. Who in your life needs to hear the Good News? Bring them with you as we kick off Holy Week and celebrate Jesus' triumphal entry into Jerusalem!

![](../images/81f7ac_b0b969c9e8e249c8913d0af88e5317d8~mv2.png)

#### Good Friday Worship @ The Garden

![](../images/81f7ac_9616fba1ca124339b04dd2570c532855~mv2.png)

Friday, April 3 · 7:00 PM · Jesus' Seven Last Words. Join us for a reverent and moving Good Friday service at The Garden of Gethsemane. A night to pause, reflect, and draw near to Christ.

#### Easter Worship 🌸

Sunday, April 5 · 10:45 AM. He is risen! Come celebrate Easter with us. Bring flowers to help decorate the cross as we worship the risen Christ together!

**Bring flowers to decorate the cross!**
