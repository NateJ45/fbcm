Building Reservation

Throughout our history and into the present, First Baptist has opened up its facilities for use by friends and community organizations.

If you would like to use our facilities for a wedding, please see our wedding page.

If you would like to use our building on a short term basis in some other way, you will need to complete a building usage form.

Please fill out an online Building Use Request Form. If the online building request form is filled out, only the signed Property Use Agreement (Release and Indemnity Agreement / Acceptance of Responsibility) must be signed and turned into the office, and you will not be required to fill out all the information about your event by hand.

Those who do not fill out the online form will need to fill out the entire building use form by hand and turn it into the Church Office, before the building use request can be considered.
