Outreach

FBCM desires to join Christ where he is already at work in our world — in Muncie and across the globe. We do so personally, by sharing the mercy and love that God has shown us with others. We also do this with community partners, stewarding our resources to support them in their work.

Ways You Can Connect With FBCM to serve our community

Volunteering with our partner organizations or at community events.

Special offerings and projects for disaster relief, mission work, and other needs.

Sewing Group (Rm 203) - A multi-aged, monthly group which sews pillowcases for those in need.

Use our building for something great! FBCM offers our facilities to host outside events and organizations. Our website has links to our building use policy here.
