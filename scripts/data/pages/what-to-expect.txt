Frequently asked questions

How do I find the church?

The church is located in downtown Muncie at 309 East Adams Street. Google maps will take you to the correct place. Our parking lot is located on the Adams Street side. The most common doors people use are the Adams Street entrance at our circular drive (wheelchair accessible), the large wooden front doors which go into the sanctuary, or the Jefferson Street side doors which enter our main hallway near the church offices.

Learn About Our Ministry

There are many areas of ongoing ministry at First Baptist Muncie, look below for more information on each!

The Worship Ministry at FBCM understands that Christian worship is a lifestyle centered around responding to the revealed God of the Bible.

Children

Children learn about the great big story of God's love each Sunday and throughout the year through age-appropriate lessons, games, and activities.

Youth

Youth encourage one another in their faith and grow in spiritual maturity together while having a lot of fun.

Adult

Adult Ministry aims to engage adults of all ages in intentional discipleship so we may follow Jesus and become more like him - full of grace and truth.

Outreach

With our ministry partners, we share the mercy and love that God has shown us with others.

We want to share the love of Christ with Muncie and the world!
