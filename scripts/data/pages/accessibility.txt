Wheelchair Accessibility

Our wheelchair accessible entrance is off our circular drive, on the Adams street side of the building.

Those wishing to use our automatic door should use the circular drive entrance.

Entrance

The sidewalk leads to the wheelchair-accessible entrance. The door opener is on the side of our mailbox, close to the door on its right.

Once inside, there is an elevator which will go to any floor: our lower level (B) which houses our fellowship hall, the first floor (where the sanctuary and offices are) or the second floor (which hosts classrooms and our youth center).

Restrooms

Wheelchair accessible restrooms, which also have baby changing tables, are on the first floor and the lower level (B).

Assisted Listening

On Sundays, if you would like an assisted listening device, please ask one of our greeters or the Welcome Center and they will be happy to give you one.
