# Review walkthrough: thirteen pages, what to look at on each

Written 2026-09-20 at the end of plan 2c. This is the page by page pass to do
before the domain moves. Everything below is on the staging address,
`https://fbcm-site.nathanjnixon86.workers.dev`, which serves the same build the
real domain will serve.

Every page has two screenshots committed in this repo, light and dark at 1280
pixels wide, taken on 2026-09-19. Open the live page first and use the
screenshot only to check that nothing has shifted since.

The intent for each page comes from the design spec,
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-fbcm-plan2-pages-design.md`, section 5.

## How to change things yourself

Everything on the site is edited at
https://fbcm-site.nathanjnixon86.workers.dev/studio. The list down the left has
six groups and nothing else, so there is no hunting:

1. **Pages.** Every page of the site, edited as the stack of bands you see on
   the page itself.
2. **Blog.** Posts and their categories.
3. **People.** Staff members, their roles, photographs, emails and bios.
4. **Ministries.** The five ministries and who to contact about each.
5. **Site settings.** The things that appear on every page: service time,
   office hours, phone, email, the announcement banner, the social links, and
   the list of old web addresses that forward visitors.
6. **Help.** "How the website works" explains the whole thing in plain
   language, and "Your church at a glance" is a scratch pad for notes.

Changes save as you type, but nothing reaches visitors until you press Publish.
Publishing rebuilds the site by itself, and the change is live in about two
minutes.

## What is measured

Speed and accessibility were measured page by page and written down in
`docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-20-lighthouse.md`: accessibility, best
practices and search readiness score 100 on every page tested, desktop speed
scores 99 to 100, and mobile speed sits at 93 to 95 with the remaining gap
coming from how the photographs are delivered.

All 44 old web addresses from the Wix site were checked against the live site
by a script anyone can re-run, `npm run verify:redirects`, and all 44 forward
to a page that answers correctly. The run is recorded in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-20-fbcm-plan2c-verification/task-4-5-report.md`.
