The journal as it ships today

Built from main at e6d1e90 with the live dataset, screenshotted at 1440 and 390 in light and dark: /blog, /blog/category/fbcm-events, /blog/tag/francis-of-assisi, and five posts (a long sermon preview, a short event, an image-heavy essay, and both posts whose event tables are bullet lists). The full audit is audit.md beside this file.

Reads well

  • The measure is on spec: body Castoro 17px / 1.72 in a 593px column, 62ch.
  • The post opener (category line, titling-capitals h1, italic lede) is the spec's "title at --text-h1, a lede from the excerpt, meta in font-ui".
  • "More from this series" is an honest ruled list that only renders when a tag is really shared.
  • Dark mode holds everywhere; nothing overflows at 390.

Fails the bar

  1. The index shows the same posts twice. Five of the six "Worth coming back for" cards are also the first cards of "All posts", eight inches lower.
  2. "This week" is eight and a half months old. The newest sermon preview is dated January 6, 2026; the band still calls it this week.
  3. The index is a wall of sermon slides. 123 of 141 covers are Wix promo graphics with their own lettering (Kingdom Family Vacation, Arise!, Multiplied); a three-up grid of them is a collage of other people's type, the opposite of "one typeface nobody else has".
  4. Blockquotes out-shout the sermon. 88 quotes render at 28px, 1.65 times the body, so a 100-word citation is the loudest thing on the page.
  5. Wix residue survives: underline marks on 405 blocks in 130 posts (headings read as links; links get a second underline); bold paragraphs doing a heading's job; Castoro has one weight, so every strong is a faux bold.
  6. Both event tables are bullet lists with the cells joined by middots: händel-s-messiah-sing-in-carols (the Messiah programme, 30 rows, with a headerless number column) and upcoming-events-at-fbc-muncie-march-3-easter-2026 (the Lent and Easter schedule, 8 rows).
  7. The lede repeats the body on 34 of 142 posts: the excerpt is the first paragraph, so the reader meets it twice in one screen.
  8. Dead anchors: the Messiah post's "Page Contents" list and three links in another post point at Wix #viewer-… fragments that do not exist here.
  9. Every post ends on three bands saying one thing: "Come and see for yourself" on indigo, then the footer poster, then the footer. The critique's "two indigo bands saying the same sentence".
  10. The contents column is empty for most of the page. A 260px sticky list beside 62ch leaves a 700px strip of paper down every long post, and on sermon previews its entries are one heading repeated three times with a longer tail.
  11. Thin archives dead-end. A one-post tag page is a heading, one card and the closing CTA; nothing offers the reader the next step.
  12. Archive navigation is Newer / Page 1 of 12 / Older: nothing says which page holds 2024.
  13. 13 posts have no category, including a sermon preview (multiplied), so the card falls back to "Blog".
  14. A cover can show twice: the Messiah post's nave photograph is both the bled cover and the first inline image, one screen apart.
  15. Two excerpts still carry em-dashes (justified-by-faith-empowered-by-the-spirit and the-road-not-taken): the converter normalised bodies, not excerpts. Rule 2 is absolute for Sanity content; it is a content fix, not a render one, and is not made here (no dataset writes).

Recommended: P2 Bulletin and I1 Register

Thesis. Three posts in four are a sermon preview, and a sermon preview is a bulletin: which Sunday, which reading, who is preaching, where to listen. The post page leads with those facts, set as a ruled order beside the title, and lets the reading open the body. The index is a register, not a gallery: it scans by date and title, the way the church's own Visitor archive and the home page's blog band already do, and the covers shrink to recognition plates because they are slides with their own lettering, not photographs.

Zero schema changes. Everything below is derived at render time from the post's own date, category, tags, body and cover dimensions (CLAUDE.md rule 15). No field is added, no document is written.

Shared reading rules (every post, every variant)

heading       h2 | h3 | h4 -> one section head: Castoro var(--h3), hairline above. (191 of 227 body headings are Wix h4s.)
underline     mark dropped everywhere. A link is already underlined; underlined non-link text reads as a link.
strong        Castoro has one weight: no faux bold. strong -> a gold highlighter band (inset box-shadow, 22%).
label para    whole paragraph strong AND <= 18 words AND (ends '?' OR starts /^\d+\.\s/ OR no end punctuation)
              -> a point: var(--item) roman; a leading "2." becomes a gold numeral line above.
              a run of labels each followed by one or more paras -> Q and A rows (the Messiah FAQ).
blockquote    Castoro italic 1.125rem (was 28px), gold 1px rule hung in the gutter so the text keeps the edge.
table         a list run of >= 3 items where EVERY item contains " · " -> a ruled table.
              cols = max cells; shorter rows padded on the LEFT (the Messiah header has no "#" column);
              row 0 is a header when every cell is <= 3 words with no digit; a lone "-" cell renders empty;
              a first column that is all numbers or empty is set in old-style gold numerals.
              Matches exactly the two posts; no other list in the 142 has a middot in every item.
dead anchors  a link whose href starts "#viewer-" -> plain text. A list whose every item is such a link
              is Wix's page-contents list -> dropped, and a trailing "Page Contents:" on the para before it too.
lede          excerpt shown UNLESS its first 60 normalised chars appear in the FIRST text paragraph (34 posts).
figure        landscape in the measure; portrait (h > w) capped at 360px; caption hangs from the 28px gold tick.
              an inline image with the SAME asset as the cover is skipped (the Messiah nave photo showed twice).
ending        Tagged (text links) -> More from this series (unchanged rule) -> two doors -> site footer.
              The journalPage closing CTA band is NOT rendered on posts: the footer poster already says it.

P2 Bulletin: the post page

isPreview     category is Sermon Preview (blog-derive.ts, unchanged)
sunday        first Sunday on or after publishedAt, in America/Indiana/Indianapolis
reading       first match of REF in the first 6 text blocks, where
              REF = (From )?BOOK \d+:\d+(-\d+)?(:\d+)?(-\d+)?    BOOK = the 66 books and common abbreviations
              95 of 105 previews match; no match -> the row is omitted, never guessed.
series        /(of|in) (our|FBCM's|the) "?([A-Z][^.,"]{3,40})"? (sermon )?series/ in the same text; 51 of 105.
listen        first link in the opening paragraph whose href contains churchcenter.com/channels.
ORDER (right column, ruled rows)
  preview     Sunday | Reading | Series | Preaching (author) | Listen        rows with no value are omitted
  other       Posted | Written by | Takes (reading time) | Filed under (category link)
LECTION       preview only: the text blocks after an opening "This is a sermon preview..." paragraph, up to and
              including the first block that contains REF (at most 4), are set as the reading: italic
              1.1875rem between two rules, "The reading" + the reference above. Text is not moved or edited.
COVER         w >= 2000 AND w/h >= 1.3 (a photograph)  -> GROUND: full bleed under the masthead (100cqw)
              otherwise (a slide: 139 of 141)              -> PLATE: matted on soft paper, gold line, in the
              right column under the order. Same classifier as the photo prototype.
right column  order, plate, then "In this post" (numbered, from the section heads, only if >= 3) , sticky.
doors         preview: "The Sunday before" / "The Sunday after", neighbours among previews, with Sunday + reading.
              other: "Newer" / "Older" by date.
phone         order, then the plate at 300px, above the body; the contents list is dropped.

I1 Register: /blog, category and tag pages

opener        h1 + lede (journalPage hero fields, unchanged) left; the DOOR right, cols 9 to 12:
  fresh       newest preview whose sunday >= today -> "This Sunday, <date>" + title + reading + author
  otherwise   newest post of any kind -> "Latest" + plate + title + excerpt
              today = build date; an inline script re-checks against the visitor's clock and swaps
              the fresh door for the latest one after its Sunday (the home hero's live-line pattern).
filters       text links with counts (derived): All, then each category with >= 1 post. The current one is
              underlined in gold. Replaces the boxed chips.
worth coming  newest 4 durable posts NOT already on page 1 (fixes the duplicate). Hidden on pages 2+.
back for
register      page items grouped by local year. Year = var(--h2) old-style numeral in cols 1-2.
              row: date (Mon D) | title + excerpt | meta | 4:3 plate
              meta: preview -> "Sunday" + Sunday date + reading;  other -> category (or nothing).
pager         prev / numbered pages / next, each number carrying the year(s) its page covers ("2025–24"), exact
              dates in its title; computed from the same paginate() the routes use. Phone: prev, "Page n of N", next.
thin state    fewer than 4 posts: "Also filed under" = the other tags and categories on those posts,
              most frequent first, max 8, then "Everything else: the whole archive".
phone         one column: meta line above the title, 84px plate at the right.
