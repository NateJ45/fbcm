# FBCM: what exists and how it got here

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Read this when you need the history of a pass (which branch, which date, which files), or the full inventory of what is built. CLAUDE.md carries only the short current state.

**What this repo is.** The First Baptist Church Muncie website (309 East Adams Street, Muncie, IN 47305), replacing the church's Wix site. Forked from `ncs-astro-sanity-starter` on 2026-09-18, so a lot of the machinery here is the starter's; this file describes FBCM as it is now. The binding spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-fbcm-rebuild-design.md`, and the art-direction spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-fbcm-art-direction-design.md`. Read the relevant one before making a judgement call about what the site should do.

**Where it stands.** Eleven pages, all composed from the page builder (`/`, `/visit`, `/who-we-are`, `/beliefs`, `/ministries`, `/staff`, `/history`, `/wedding`, `/give`, `/contact`, `/blog`), each seeded by a module under `scripts/pages/`, a twelfth, `/visitor` (applied 2026-09-24), and a thirteenth, `/architecture` (applied 2026-10-03: the old Wix page brought back for the building-upkeep appeal, kept out of the header menu, in the footer, and also reached from History's 1921 to 1929 band and the Give page; `scripts/pages/architecture.mjs`, with `scripts/link-architecture.mjs` as the targeted patch that added those two links). 142 imported posts at their original `/post/<slug>` URLs with restored bodies, 17 staff, 5 ministries, 48 retired-URL redirects. The site draws no form of its own: visitors use the church's own forms, linked, or embedded from Church Trac through the Church Trac form band. The art-direction pass (merged 2026-09-21 at `183a61f`) rebuilt the identity: Castoro Titling for display capitals, Castoro for reading, Sofia Sans Semi Condensed for furniture, photographs used as layout through a shared spare-image pool (`src/lib/spare-images.ts`), a Stone Steps-style header, footer and mobile menu, and every section redrawn in hairlines and asymmetric grids, with zero Sanity schema changes. Five branches were integrated to `main` on 2026-09-22 (`integrate/2026-09-23`). The RichText Ledger and photo-shapes pass replaced that pass's remaining monoculture: `RichTextSection` now lays its body out from the body's own shape (`src/lib/rich-shape.ts`) and `ImageText` now places its photograph by the photograph's own shape under a one-window, two-ground page budget (`src/lib/photo-shape.ts`), with one deliberate schema addition (section bodies now accept `h4`, "Small heading"). The journal polish redrew the post page as a sermon bulletin ("P2 Bulletin", `src/pages/post/[slug].astro`, with `src/lib/post-body.ts`, `sermon-derive.ts` and `post-neighbours.ts`) and the blog index and archives as a dated register ("I1 Register", `src/components/blog/`), with zero schema changes. The Studio pass added the Help guides (`src/sanity/guides/`), a readability pass over the desk, Site settings placeholders (`{time}`, `{address}` and six more, filled at the two fetch chokepoints by `src/lib/settings-placeholders.ts`, so the service time, address, phone and email live only in Site settings), and the Ministry band: a `ministrySection` points at a `ministry` document and `SectionRenderer` resolves it to the ImageText or RichText band it draws as (`src/lib/ministry-band.ts`), with `scripts/connect-ministries.mjs` as the backup-first migration that points the Ministries page at the documents. The Who We Are identity pass (branch `feat/who-we-are-alive`, 2026-09-23) added the church primitives in `src/components/church/` (arch frames, building glyphs, the watchword mark), a `rule` button variant (an alias of the header's gold GIVE plate since the same day), and four church blocks (Watchword, Goals, Pledge, Letter) plus an indigo `window` hero and arched-door link cards, all in the church's brand colours only (`--color-band-*`; the owner dropped the church-year season the same day); `scripts/pages/who-we-are.mjs` is composed from them and waits on a deploy before `--apply`. The Home identity pass (branch `feat/home-identity`, 2026-09-23) composed the home page from those church blocks (`scripts/pages/home.mjs`, also waiting on a deploy): the Sunday times as a brown hymn board, the goals as glyphed doors, a dated cream Our Building band ending in the present, the journal as taupe blog rows and a gold give band, with every church band h2 in one heading grammar (`src/lib/heading-grammar.ts`). The journal identity pass (branch `feat/journal-identity`, 2026-09-24) carried those rows into /blog, the archives and the post page (`src/components/blog/PostRow.astro`): an indigo opener, taupe bands, covers in arches with no captions, brand inks throughout. The History identity pass (branch `feat/history-identity`, 2026-09-24) gave the heritage band a third form, the page opener (`HeritageOpener.astro`: brown, the derived span "1859 to <build year>", the church's own "Our History", two old photographs in a door and a lancet), and recomposed `scripts/pages/history.mjs` with a timeline that ends in a "Today" row; it waits on a deploy before `--apply`. The Beliefs identity pass (branch `feat/beliefs-identity`, 2026-09-24) put the RichText body in the identity: prose in one reading measure, and a text band on the cadence's muted turn drawn as a brand band derived from its neighbours (`src/lib/rich-ground.ts`); `scripts/pages/beliefs.mjs` waits on a deploy before `--apply`. The craft-details pass (branch `feat/details`, 2026-09-24) gave every page and post a branded share card drawn at build time, replaced the starter's LocalBusiness with a Church node, the weekly service as an Event and a richer BlogPosting (validated offline by `src/lib/schema-vocab.ts`), and put a "Which door?" street-side sketch in Visit's doors band (`src/components/church/DoorPlan.astro`). The scripture index and site search (branch `feat/scripture-search`, 2026-09-24) added `/blog/scripture` (every passage preached, derived from the posts) and a Pagefind site search in the site's own dialog, loaded only when opened. The print and motion pass (branch `feat/print-motion`, 2026-09-24) made a post print as a bulletin (`@media print` in the post page's own style), made the building glyphs draw themselves once in view and the Hannaford rendering ink in, carried a blog row's title into the post's h1 as a shared view transition, stopped the page sliding during the cross-fade, and removed Lenis (scrolling is native; rule 5). The scripture text pass (branch `feat/scripture-text`, 2026-09-24) opened every sermon preview's reading into its passage (a `<details>` in the Berean Standard Bible, public domain, fetched at build time by `scripts/fetch-scripture.mjs` and cached in `node_modules/.cache/scripture/`; the NIV through API.Bible when `API_BIBLE_KEY` is set, capped at Biblica's allowance), and gave posts a quiet tools row: Read aloud (Web Speech), Share (Web Share, else copy) and, on a preview, Add to calendar (a static `/post/<slug>/sunday.ics` from the reusable `src/lib/ics.ts`). The Last Sunday pass (branch `feat/last-sunday`, 2026-09-24) put the latest Sunday service recording on Home, read at build time from the channel's public YouTube feed and placed from code through a `SectionRenderer` slot (`src/components/home/LastSunday.astro`, `src/lib/youtube-feed.ts`), a browser-fetched National Weather Service line for Sunday on Visit, and a weekly `/visit/sunday.ics`; `deploy.yml` now also rebuilds on Sunday afternoon and Monday morning. The This Sunday pass (branch `feat/this-sunday-youtube`, 2026-09-24) gave the hero's dated line a second sermon source, since the church stopped writing previews in January 2026: with no preview for the coming Sunday it names the sermon of the broadcast already scheduled on YouTube for that Sunday, linking to the watch page (`src/lib/this-sunday.ts`, `upcomingBroadcast()` in `youtube-feed.ts`), and `deploy.yml` also rebuilds on Thursday and Saturday mornings so the line picks the broadcast up. The preacher pass (branch `feat/preacher-and-feel`, 2026-09-25) added the coming Sunday's preacher as a fourth hero fact (`src/lib/preacher.ts`: the preview's author, else the broadcast's "Preaching:" line, server-rendered hidden and shown only until its Sunday has passed) and one schema field, `heroFact.note`, for a small line under the Sunday time; `scripts/pages/home.mjs` proposes "Intergenerational, casual dress welcome", written only once `SERVICE_NOTE_APPROVED` is true, after the church approves it. The Visitor pass (branch `feat/the-visitor`, 2026-09-24) gave the church newsletter its own page, `/visitor` (`scripts/pages/visitor.mjs`: the issues as a document list the page draws as the latest issue large and a wall of covers by year, `VisitorIssues.astro`; the two books below), covers drawn from page 1 of each PDF at build time with pdfjs (`scripts/visitor-covers.mjs`, cached in `node_modules/.cache/visitor/`, no upload), a band on Home for the newest issue (enlarged in `feat/visitor-band`, 2026-09-25: the church's own description, the age derived from the year in the /visitor page's own line, the newest cover with the two before it fanned behind, `src/lib/visitor-band.ts`), a footer link through the page's own "Show in the footer" switch, and every issue's words in the site search as Pagefind custom records; `/blog` keeps one line anchored `#publications`. No schema change; `/styleguide/visitor` shows the composed page until the apply. The What's On pass (2026-09-25) added `/events`, the church calendar read at build time from Church Trac's iCal feed and drawn in the identity (`src/lib/church-calendar.ts`, `church-calendar-feed.ts`; each row a `/events/<key>.ics` and a schema.org Event), with Church Trac the one place an event is typed, and a Home band for the next three events after The Visitor (`src/components/home/WhatsOnBand.astro`); the feed's times are read as Muncie's clock whatever zone it names, since the Church Trac account says Halifax. The Church Trac forms pass (2026-09-25) added a `churchTracForm` document (a form's name, the embed code pasted from Church Trac, and a size) and a `churchTracFormSection` band in the Church group that points at one (`src/components/sections/ChurchTracForm.astro`): the pasted code is never rendered, `src/lib/church-trac-form.ts` keeps only an https churchtrac.com iframe address from it (the same check validates the Studio's paste box), and the band draws its own frame, words beside it and an "Open the form in a new tab" line; `/styleguide` shows it with the public calendar standing in until the real embed codes arrive. The ministry newsletters pass (2026-09-25) added `/kids-corner` and `/youth-news`: the Children's and Youth ministries' newsletters (The Kid's Corner, The Moose's Message), which they write as Church Trac pages, read from Church Trac's HTML at build time (`src/lib/church-trac-page.ts`, a small tokenizer, no dependency; `src/lib/church-trac-newsletters.ts`) and drawn in the identity (`src/components/newsletter/Newsletter.astro`), with Church Trac's template text, hidden and unpublished parts left out, a derived link on the Children and Youth ministry bands and in the footer, and a "Read it on Church Trac" fallback. The hero motion pass (branch `feat/hero-motion`, 2026-09-27) made the photo hero move: up to twelve frames (schema cap raised from six) on long and short beats with a push-in, drift or pull-out on every frame, a small sequencer that inserts frames one ahead, a CSS scroll parallax, a dark backing behind a slideshow hero's words (so every word holds contrast over all twelve frames), and the headline's words rising one by one on the photo and split heroes (`src/lib/hero-frames.ts` `frameBeats`, `src/lib/hero-words.ts`); `scripts/pages/home.mjs` lists the twelve frames, applied 2026-09-28 (the service line is held back behind `SERVICE_NOTE_APPROVED` until the church approves it). The photo library's binary manifest and gap-fill data live in `scripts/data/`. See `docs/agent/components.md`. The openers' motion pass (branch `feat/motion-openers`, 2026-09-28) carried the home hero's word rise to every page-opening h1 through one renderer, `src/components/RiseWords.astro` (the window hero with its closing accent line, SectionHeading's h1, the journal Opener, What's On, the ministry newsletters and The Visitor), except the post page, whose title is the shared view transition, and gave a single-photo hero one 5 s push-in towards its hotspot (`.hero-still`), short enough that WCAG 2.2.2 asks for no Pause; no page draws that branch today, so `/styleguide` shows it. It is deployed at https://fbcm-site.nathanjnixon86.workers.dev. **Next is plan 3, the cutover** of `fbcmuncie.org` (`docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-20-cutover-plan.md`), which waits on Nathan's decisions listed in `docs/PENDING.md`.
